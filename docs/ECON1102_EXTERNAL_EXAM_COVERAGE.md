Prepared 12 August 2026 from the supplied external-exam brief and the live
`agent/initial-import` checkout after PR #8 merged.

canonical JSON deck, the two question-bank JSON files, the explicit
`knowledge/card-concept-map.json`, the 297 knowledge records, and the Guided
Knowledge Check registry were inspected locally. A phrase search was not used
as the coverage test: a family is covered when the current course model is
retrievable from the mapped concepts/cards/questions, even if the historical
source used different wording.

## Source taxonomy used

The skill families below were derived from the supplied brief. External source
URLs and their intended reliability hierarchy are recorded in
[`ECON1102_EXAM_PRIORITY_AUDIT.md`](./ECON1102_EXAM_PRIORITY_AUDIT.md). The
local course sources were treated as authoritative for model conventions.

Ratings mean `strong` = directly retrievable and practised in the current
bundle, `partial` = components exist but the externally observed integrated
discrimination is undertrained, and `absent` = no adequate current retrieval
path was found. `Action taken` describes the decision at this pre-addition
baseline; the final delta is recorded at the end of this file.

+## Corrected immutable exam-yield attribution audit

The registry distinguishes concepts that constitute an examinable skill from concepts that merely support its explanation. `targetConceptIds` are the only concept IDs allowed to originate a skill's direct yield. `supportingConceptIds` are indexed for audit/disclosure context but do not receive the skill's full direct yield. `cardIds` are the authoritative direct retrieval assets; a shared broad concept does not make an unlisted card a direct card.

Source relationships are explicit: `direct` means the exact skill or mechanism is visible; `family` means a related mechanism is visible; `scope` and `format` provide current-course or format context. The learner-facing direct-final label is emitted only for an explicit `direct` relationship.

## Candidate-gap source checks

### Trade-weighted index

The current local bundle supports this skill: Week 8 Lecture 2 includes a
real-TWI/net-exports figure, and the required textbook’s international chapter
contains the Australia TWI section. The absence was therefore a missing
retrieval node, not evidence that an old practice-set noun should be imported.

### Money destruction

The current Week 5 lecture and textbook explain that calling in loans is a bank
option and show the loan/deposit balance-sheet process. The canonical
`money-creation` concept already states the reverse repayment implication, but
the learner had no standalone card or question requiring the distinction among
new lending, repayment, default/write-off, and an interbank transfer. This was
rated partial rather than absent because the current model is already present.

### Cash rate and short-term securities

The current Week 7 lecture and textbook explicitly link the cash rate to
short-term and longer-term rates through expectations and premia; the deck
already covers cash rate, yield curve, bond price, and OMO separately. The
missing part was the integrated reasoning chain from the required return on a
short security to demand/price/yield. This was rated partial and limited to a
single analogous retrieval addition.

## Integrated-chain audit conclusion

The bank-risk, wage-floor-policy, and PRF/deflation/ZLB families had adequate
components but no sufficiently direct integrated MCQ in the baseline bank.
AD-AS self-correction, supply-shock trade-off, small-open fiscal, and most FX
regime chains already had mixed or sequence questions. The implementation adds
only the narrow partial/absent paths listed in the final delta below.
